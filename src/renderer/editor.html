How to Use This Chart

Placing Stamps

Pick a color from the Stamp row, then click a day's Stamp cell to apply it.

  • Red — Menstruation / bleeding
  • Yellow — Same discharge pattern
  • Green — Dry / infertile
  • Yellow Baby — Fertile discharge
  • White Baby — Peak-type mucus
  • Green Baby — Green / fertile

Peak & Fertility Markers

Click P, 1, 2, 3, or I, then click a day's Stamp cell to toggle that marker on or off. A day can carry one stamp color and one marker at the same time.

  • P — Peak day
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — 1st, 2nd, 3rd fertile day after Peak
  • I — Intercourse

Correction Mode

Turn on Correction before making a change to mark it as a professional correction rather than the client's original entry — corrected stamps, markers, dates, and descriptions appear tilted or in red. Turn it back off to return to normal entries.

Erasing

Click ✕, then click a day's Stamp cell to clear its stamp and marker (clears the correction layer instead if Correction is on).

Date & Description

Type directly into a day's Date or Description cell. Press Tab / Shift+Tab to move to the next or previous day.

Text Boxes

Click Text Box, then click anywhere on the chart to drop a note. Drag it by its top edge to move it, and hover over it to reveal the delete button.

Rows, Zoom & Saving

+ Add Cycle Row adds another 35-day cycle to the chart. Zoom with the toolbar controls or ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0. ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z undo and redo. ⌘S saves the chart.
