How to Use This Chart

Placing Stamps

Pick a color from the Stamp row, then click anywhere on the photo to place it there.

  • Red — Menstruation / bleeding
  • Yellow — Same discharge pattern
  • Green — Dry / infertile
  • Yellow Baby — Fertile discharge
  • White Baby — Peak-type mucus
  • Green Baby — Green / fertile

Peak & Fertility Markers

Click P, 1, 2, 3, or I, then click anywhere on the photo to place it.

  • P — Peak day
  • 1 / 2 / 3 — 1st, 2nd, 3rd day after Peak
  • I — Intercourse

Moving, Resizing & Deleting Stamps

Switch to Pan mode (the hand icon) to drag empty space and scroll the photo. In Pan mode, click an existing stamp to select it — drag it to move, press Delete/Backspace to remove it, or use the size control to resize it.

Stamp Size

The size box shows the selected stamp's size, or the size new stamps will use if nothing's selected. Click −/+ to adjust by 10, or type an exact size (20–200).

Correction Mode

Turn on Correction before placing a stamp, marker, or text to mark it as a professional correction rather than the client's original entry — corrected items appear tilted or in red. Turn it back off to return to normal entries.

Erasing

Click ✕, then click directly on a stamp or marker on the photo to remove it (removes its correction layer instead if Correction is on).

Text Boxes

Click Text Box, then click anywhere on the photo to drop a note.

Photo, Zoom & Saving

Upload Photo replaces the chart's background image without affecting your stamps or notes. Zoom with the toolbar controls or ⌘+ / ⌘− / ⌘0. ⌘Z and ⌘⇧Z undo and redo. ⌘S saves the chart.
